How it works

A ranking model: it does not guess prices, it orders stocks by how they are likely to do relative to the market. The model design, inputs and settings are proprietary.

1. Market data

Price and volume history, corporate events and market structure, each as it was known that day.

2. AI ensemble

Several complementary models trained on the whole market, retrained on a fixed schedule and tested before every change.

3. Daily ranking

One score per stock for about 4,000 NSE stocks, 1, 5 and 20 sessions ahead.

4. Freeze and prove

Frozen before the open, SHA-256 fingerprint published, file revealed about a month later so anyone can verify it.

Live track record

Live results matter more than any backtest, so they are published as they come, good or bad.

Paper trading since 17 Nov 2026: -3.3 % after 5 sessions

5 longs + 5 shorts a day, Rs 1 lakh, real prices and costs. A poor start: only 2.3 % of 5-day stretches in the backtest were this bad. A test of a liquidity floor and a stop-loss on 2024-25 data did not improve the book, so the rules stay unchanged. A fair verdict needs 20 to 30 sessions (late December).

Shadow test of the next version: 1 scored day

The next version runs every night next to the live model without changing anything published. A switch needs at least 10 scored days.

Forecast proof: how to verify a forecast

Every forecast's SHA-256 fingerprint is committed to the repository before the next market open. About a month later the exact file is published; run sha256sum on it and compare. Details.

Why trust these numbers?

Every experiment's pass / fail rule is written before it runs, no model is scored on dates it trained on, costs are in every money result, statistics account for day-to-day dependence, and failures are counted in the experiment record.
